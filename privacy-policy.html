🛡️ 1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

Welcome to PR Recipe Hub ("we," "our," or "us"). We provide a comprehensive culinary recipe discovery, meal planning, pantry ingredient management, and smart cooking timer application for Android and iOS devices.

Offline-First Principle: PR Recipe Hub is architected with a strict offline-first privacy foundation . Your personal recipes, saved favorites, grocery shopping lists, weekly meal calendars, custom tags, and pantry stock are saved exclusively on your local device. We do not maintain user accounts, require logins, or store your private culinary data on remote cloud databases.

By installing, accessing, or using the PR Recipe Hub mobile application (the "Application"), you acknowledge and agree to the data collection and usage practices described in this Privacy Policy.

💾 2. Information Collection & Storage Architecture

A. Local Device Storage (AsyncStorage)

All user-generated activities and customizations remain 100% on your device, including:

  • Favorite & Custom Recipe Bookmarks: Your saved culinary collections.
  • Pantry Inventory: Stock levels, ingredient names, and categories you manage.
  • Weekly Meal Planner: Breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snack meal schedules.
  • Smart Grocery Shopping List: Merged ingredient quantities and item check states.
  • Dietary & Allergy Preferences: Vegetarian/vegan preferences, allergy exclusions, and metric/imperial measurement selections.
  • Cooking History & Notes: Timers, step completions, and custom notes.

B. Public Recipe Data API (TheMealDB) & CDN Images

The Application fetches public culinary recipes, category catalogs, and ingredient imagery from TheMealDB API (https://www.themealdb.com) via secure HTTPS requests. No personal identification data (PII) is transmitted in these requests.

C. Anonymous Diagnostics & Stability Metrics

To ensure high application reliability, non-identifying technical crash reports and system metrics (such as operating system version, device model, and fatal error stack traces) may be processed transiently. These logs contain no personal user data.

⚙️ 3. Specific Feature Privacy Protections

A. 19-Language Localization & Dynamic Translation

PR Recipe Hub supports 19 languages (English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Turkish, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Kannada). Recipe translation and dictionary lookups operate using locally bundled language dictionaries and client-side translation caching without profiling your identity.

B. Smart Pantry Ingredient Matcher

The algorithmic matching engine that identifies recipes you can cook based on your available pantry items executes entirely on your device's local CPU. No pantry ingredient inventories are uploaded to cloud servers.

C. Smart Local Notifications & Alarms

All reminders (meal prep alarms, breakfast/lunch/dinner scheduling, and cooking timer chimes) are scheduled strictly through local device notification queues. We do not operate remote push servers or track notification engagement for advertising profiling.

D. Cooking Mode & Screen Keep-Awake

During active step-by-step cooking, the app may request temporary screen-wake locks to keep your recipe visible while your hands are busy. No ambient audio or visual recordings are captured during cooking sessions.

📢 4. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing development and keep PR Recipe Hub free for users worldwide, the Application displays advertisements through Google AdMob and authorized partner networks.

A. Google Ad Formats Served

  • Adaptive Banners: Non-intrusive banner displays positioned at the screen bottom.
  • Native Ad Cards: Contextually styled recipe recommendation cards in browse feeds.
  • Interstitial Ads: Natural transition ads displayed between screen navigations.
  • Rewarded Video Ads: Optional user-initiated ads that unlock bonus culinary features.

B. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) & GDPR/CCPA Compliance

  • EEA / UK Consent: For users in the European Economic Area and UK, explicit consent for personalized ads is gathered via the certified Google UMP dialog before ads are requested.
  • US State Privacy (CCPA/CPRA): California and other applicable US residents can opt out of personalized ad targeting at any time.
  • In-App Consent Controls: You can review, update, or revoke advertising preferences at any time under Settings > Security & Privacy > Monetization & Ad Preferences.
  • Non-Personalized Ads: If you decline personalized advertising, Google AdMob serves contextual ads that do not use cookies or mobile advertising IDs for user profiling.

Safety Context Protection: PR Recipe Hub enforces strict safety rules that block interstitial ads during active Cooking Mode, active countdown timers, and kitchen safety sessions to prevent distraction during hot meal preparation.

👶 5. Children's Privacy & COPPA Compliance

PR Recipe Hub is designed for a general audience and strictly complies with the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • Child-Directed Treatment Tag: The app includes a "Child-Directed Treatment" toggle under privacy settings that configures the Google AdMob SDK to disable interest-based ads and remarketing.
  • No Data Collection on Minors: We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately for removal.

🔑 6. System Permissions Requested

PR Recipe Hub requests only the minimal set of operating system permissions required for core culinary features:

Permission Platform Technical Purpose & Necessity
INTERNET Android Fetches public recipes, category data, and displays sponsor ads via Google AdMob.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Android Detects online/offline connectivity to activate local offline recipe caching.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Android 13+ Delivers local cooking timer alerts and user-scheduled meal reminders.
VIBRATE Android Provides tactile feedback when a cooking timer completes.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Android Restores user-scheduled local meal reminders after a device reboot.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID Android Permits Google AdMob to deliver relevant advertisements in compliance with Google Play policy.

Sensitive Permissions Not Requested: We do not request or require access to your camera, microphone, device contacts, fine/coarse GPS location, or personal photo library.

⚖️ 7. User Rights, Data Portability & Deletion

In accordance with global privacy regulations (including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and LGPD), you have complete control over your data:

  • Data Portability & Export: You can export a comprehensive JSON archive containing all your saved favorites, weekly meal plans, pantry inventory, and shopping lists at any time via Settings > Storage & Privacy > Export Backup .
  • Cache Clearance: You can clear cached recipe images and network payloads without losing your custom favorites.
  • Complete Data Erasure (Factory Reset): You can wipe all locally stored application data instantly using the Clear All Data button in settings.
