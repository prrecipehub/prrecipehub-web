Paneer Butter Masala

Main Course

Rich, creamy, restaurant-style North Indian curry made with succulent cubes of fresh paneer simmered in a velvety tomato-cashew butter gravy.

Step-by-Step Cooking Guidance:
1
Cut 300g fresh paneer into 1-inch cubes and soak in warm water with a pinch of salt for 10 minutes to keep them ultra-soft.
2
In a saucepan, boil 4 chopped ripe tomatoes, 1 chopped red onion, 2 green cardamoms, 1 inch cinnamon stick, 15 whole cashew nuts, 1 inch ginger, and 4 garlic cloves in 1 cup water for 10 minutes.
3
Transfer boiled mixture to a blender and grind into a silky-smooth, vibrant orange-red puree; strain through a fine sieve for a velvet restaurant texture.
